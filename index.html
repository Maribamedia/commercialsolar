Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about commercial solar solutions

Q

How is the 70% electricity bill reduction guaranteed?

We conduct a comprehensive energy audit analyzing 12-24 months of your electricity bills. Using advanced simulation software, we model your system's performance based on your location, roof orientation, and consumption patterns. The 70% reduction is contractually guaranteed based on this detailed analysis, with performance metrics and reporting provided monthly.

Q

What happens during loadshedding with a solar system?

Our commercial systems include battery storage specifically sized for your critical loads. During loadshedding, essential systems (lighting, security, refrigeration, etc.) automatically switch to battery power without interruption. The system is designed to prioritize critical circuits to ensure business continuity throughout power outages.

Q

How long does the installation process take?

Most commercial installations are completed within 45-75 days from contract signing. This includes: design and engineering (10-14 days), municipal approvals (7-14 days), equipment procurement (7-14 days), and installation (7-21 days). We work around your business hours to minimize disruption, often completing installations during weekends or after hours.

Q

What maintenance is required for the solar system?

Solar systems require minimal maintenance. Our comprehensive package includes: bi-annual professional cleaning and inspection, 24/7 remote monitoring, and immediate response to any performance issues. All maintenance is included in our service agreement, and we provide monthly performance reports showing your actual savings versus projections.

Q

What financing options are available with zero upfront cost?

We offer three primary financing models: 1) Rent-to-Own - fixed monthly payments leading to ownership; 2) Power Purchase Agreement - pay only for solar energy used; 3) Asset Financing - traditional loan with immediate ownership. All options require zero capital investment upfront, and we guarantee your monthly payment will be less than your electricity savings.

Q

How does solar affect my property value?

Commercial properties with solar systems typically see a 20-25% increase in valuation. This is due to: 1) Reduced operating expenses (increased NOI), 2) Energy independence during loadshedding, 3) Attractive feature for environmentally-conscious tenants, 4) Protection against future electricity price increases. The system becomes a revenue-generating asset on your balance sheet.

Q

What happens if my electricity consumption changes?

Our systems are designed with scalability in mind. If your consumption increases (new tenants, equipment additions, expansion), we can easily add additional panels and battery capacity. Conversely, if consumption decreases, you can redirect excess energy back to the grid through net metering arrangements with your municipality, potentially generating additional revenue.

Q

What warranties and guarantees do you provide?

We provide comprehensive guarantees: 1) Performance Guarantee - 70% electricity reduction or we compensate the difference; 2) Equipment Warranty - 25 years on panels, 10 years on inverters; 3) Workmanship Warranty - 10 years on installation; 4) Service Guarantee - 24/7 support with 48-hour response time. All backed by insurance and manufacturer guarantees.
